BALLI FATTA
RASSI

A high-fidelity simulation of the PGT obstacle — the iconic pole-tied-with-rope structure. Master the rules, engineer solutions, and earn your OLQ rating under a live countdown.

01
The Balli Fatta Rassi
A bamboo pole (balli) lashed with rope (rassi) to create the load carrier. It cannot touch the ground between Start and End banks.
02
Out-of-Bounds
Sand/water between banks is Out of Bounds (OOB). No person or equipment may touch the OOB area at any time.
03
Equipment Rules
Plank, rope, and pole must be used to bridge the gap. Red-marked structures are out-of-bounds for resting equipment.
04
Team Leadership
The commander delegates roles. One person acts as load-carrier anchor, others act as stabilisers and guides.
